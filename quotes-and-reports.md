# Quotes, kilograms sold and reports

## Quotes and kilograms sold

1. **Product library.** Each product has a price and kilograms per unit.
2. **Quote line items.** Sales picks products and enters a quantity.
3. **Kilograms sold.** Quantity multiplied by kilograms per unit on won deals, summed by chemical and by year.

Keep one unit field so kilograms and tonnes do not mix.

## Reports for the Sales Manager

| Report | Question it answers |
|---|---|
| Lead response | How many leads came in, and how many were not touched within 1 day? |
| MQL to SQL | What is the conversion by month and by rep? |
| Stuck deals | Which deals have been in a stage for over 2 weeks? |
| Samples | How many samples were requested and sent? |
| Late samples | Which samples are past the 2-day target? |
| Kg sold | How many kilograms were sold, by chemical and by year? |
